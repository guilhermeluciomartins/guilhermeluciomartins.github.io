Skills

Throughout my academic career I have taken part in a range of studies that shaped a broad methodological portfolio, spanning soil and environmental chemistry, molecular microbial ecology, bioinformatics, and applied statistics.

Soil chemistry and fertility

  • Soil fertility analysis: plant-available macro- and micronutrients by ion-exchange resin and DTPA extraction (van Raij et al., 1997; EMBRAPA methods).
  • Soil phosphorus fractionation (Hedley et al., 1982): sequential extraction separating labile, moderately labile, organic, non-labile and residual P pools.
  • Soil carbon fractionation into particulate organic matter (POM) and mineral-associated organic matter (MAOM) following Cambardella & Elliott (1992).
  • Total C and N and their stable isotopes (δ¹³C, δ¹⁵N) in soil and plant samples.

Dissolved organic matter

  • Extraction and quantification of dissolved organic carbon (DOC).
  • Dissolved organic matter (DOM) isolation by solid-phase extraction (PPL cartridges) and molecular characterization by high-resolution mass spectrometry.
  • Molecular formula assignment with the MFAssignR package and compositional classification of DOM using van Krevelen diagrams.

Organic residues and fertilizers

  • Composting of organic residues, including animal manures and agro-industrial by-products.
  • Characterization of organic fertilizers following USEPA methods 3051A and 6010: pH; total organic carbon by dry combustion and wet oxidation (Walkley–Black); total nitrogen by Kjeldahl digestion; labile macronutrients (P, K, Ca, Mg, S) and micronutrients (Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu, B) by calcium-acetate and microwave-assisted digestion.

Plant analyses

  • Total carbon and nitrogen in plant tissue by dry combustion.
  • Nitric–perchloric acid digestion of plant tissue for elemental quantification.

Soil biology and microbial ecology (bench)

  • DNA extraction from soil, litter, plant tissue and organic residues.
  • Phospholipid fatty acid (PLFA) extraction and assignment to microbial functional groups.
  • Soil enzyme activity assays: β-glucosidase (Eivazi & Tabatabai, 1988), acid phosphatase and arylsulfatase (Tabatabai & Bremner, 1969, 1970).
  • Microcosm incubation experiments on pesticide mineralization using a dilution-to-extinction approach with ¹⁴C-labelled molecules.

Bioinformatics

  • Amplicon sequencing analysis of the 16S rRNA gene and the fungal ITS region (DADA2 pipeline).
  • Functional prediction from amplicon data with PICRUSt2.
  • Fungal functional guild assignment with FUNGuild and FungalTraits.
  • Shotgun metagenomics: quality control (FastQC, Trimmomatic), read merging (PEAR), contig assembly (MEGAHIT), ORF prediction (Prodigal), taxonomic annotation (Kraken2) and functional annotation against biogeochemical-cycle databases, including PCycDB (phosphorus) and dbCAN (carbohydrate-active enzymes).

Biostatistics and data analysis

  • Univariate analysis: ANOVA, Pearson and Spearman correlation.
  • Multivariate and community analysis: PERMANOVA, ordination (PCoA, NMDS, RDA), alpha-diversity indices and co-occurrence network analysis.
  • Differential abundance analysis: ALDEx2, LinDA and MaAsLin2.
  • Modelling: linear mixed-effects models (LMM), random forest for regression and classification, structural equation modelling (piecewiseSEM, lavaan) and partial least squares path modelling (PLS-PM).

Experimental design and field work

  • Greenhouse experiments with maize, soybean and tree seedlings.
  • Experimental design and analysis for completely randomized and randomized complete block designs.
  • Planning and execution of field sampling campaigns for soil, litter and plant material.

Analytical instrumentation

  • ICP-OES — multi-element quantification in digested organic fertilizers and residues.
  • Atomic absorption spectrophotometry (AAS) — macro- and micronutrients in soil extracts and digested plant tissue.
  • EA-IRMS (Delta Plus) — total C and N with stable isotope ratios (δ¹³C, δ¹⁵N) in soil and plant samples.
  • LECO elemental analyzer — total C and N by dry combustion.
  • High-resolution mass spectrometry (Orbitrap Elite) — molecular-level characterization of dissolved organic matter.
  • GC-FID and GC-MS — PLFA quantification and peak identification.
  • TOC-VCPN (Shimadzu) — dissolved and total organic carbon.
  • Liquid scintillation spectroscopy — ¹⁴CO₂ quantification in radioisotope-based mineralization assays.
  • Microwave-assisted digestion systems — sample preparation under USEPA 3051A.
  • UV-Vis spectrophotometry — colorimetric endpoints in soil enzyme assays and phosphorus fractionation.
